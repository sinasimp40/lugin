description: Voice quality expectations for Denfi announcement samples and future implementation.

Member names must be easy to hear in announcements. The owner rejected the basic local voice and the slower follow-up sample: the beginning of the member name was not audible, pronunciation was poor, and the voice lacked emotion. They requested a free voice with emotion.

**Why:** The owner gave this feedback after listening to both free HTML voice tests.

**How to apply:** Audition complete member names, including their first syllables, before selecting an engine. Do not keep offering speed-only adjustments as a solution to the rejected voice. Research free expressive engines and verify licensing and resource needs; do not promise that a new engine will pronounce a name correctly without a listening test.

For the voice test, the owner chose Kokoro Heart at normal 1× speed. The required spoken pronunciation of the shop name is “DEN PIE,” not “Den-fee.”

**Why:** The owner specified this voice and explicitly corrected the agent's mistaken assumption that “DEN PIE” was an unwanted pronunciation.

**How to apply:** Keep Heart at normal 1× speed. Use “DEN PIE” as the spoken brand while retaining written Denfi branding; do not infer speech from its written spelling. Check the shop name separately from member names. The owner has explicitly authorized production integration into the Windows executable.

Voice should start promptly with a ready-made Heart opening sentence while personalized details are prepared. Never delay locking the PC or completing a betting transaction just to wait for speech.

**Why:** The owner reported several-second login, logout, and betting announcement delays and requested real-time voice. Newly generated arbitrary text still needs CPU time; prerecorded openings remove that wait from the start of speech.

**How to apply:** Keep all audio offline and at 1×. Preparation must remain silent, private results must stay behind the reel reveal, and public wins must originate from the shared authoritative feed. Explicit matching logout can announce immediately without weakening automatic logout confirmation. Do not promise zero latency for LAN/device delays or overlapping announcements.
