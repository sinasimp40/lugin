---
name: Free voice hosting limits
description: Availability and offline expectations when comparing open-source announcement engines.
---

Free, commercially usable model licenses do not imply unlimited free hosted inference. The public Chatterbox ZeroGPU demo accepted a few anonymous generations, then required more quota/authentication. Kokoro's public demo also offers CPU generation, independent of its GPU quota.

**Why:** A sample comparison encountered Chatterbox's anonymous hosting limit before every announcement could be generated. Treating free engines as unlimited hosted APIs would mislead the shop owner.

**How to apply:** Provide saved, clearly identified audio for reliable comparisons; show live-demo failures explicitly, never substitute Windows speech. State that saved clips can play offline while new text needs either an available hosted engine or a separately installed local engine. Do not promise offline dynamic announcements before checking the actual shop hardware.
